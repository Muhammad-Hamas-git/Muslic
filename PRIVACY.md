# müslic privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

müslic is a music player made by dot Studios. It plays audio files that are
already stored on your phone.

## What the app accesses

- **Audio files on your phone.** müslic asks for permission to read your
  music so it can list and play it. Files are read on your phone only.
- **Notifications.** müslic asks for permission to show a notification so
  its playback controls appear in the notification shade and on the lock
  screen.
- **Vibration.** Used for a light tick while scrolling the library (can be
  turned off in Settings).

## What the app stores

All of this stays on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

- your settings (sorting, folder rules, equalizer, speed and pitch)
- the last song you played and where you stopped, if "Remember last song
  playing" is on
- small copies of album art, used for the carousel and blurred backgrounds

Uninstalling müslic deletes all of it. You can also clear the artwork copies
from Settings at any time.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, upload or share any personal data.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not connect to the internet.
- It does not change your music files. Tidied song names are only shown in
  the app.
